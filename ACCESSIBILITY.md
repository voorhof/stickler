# Accessibility Statement

Stickler is committed to making its public website and Filament admin panel accessible to as many people as possible, including people with disabilities.

## Our Commitment

We aim to meet the [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) at level AA. Accessibility is considered during design, development, and review.

## Measures Taken

- A "Skip to main content" link is available on every page.
- The public frontend is built with Bootstrap, which provides semantic HTML and keyboard-accessible components.
- Images uploaded through the Media Library can carry alternative text.
- The Filament admin panel uses its built-in accessible components.

## Supported Environments

- Current versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge.
- Desktop and mobile browsers.
- Screen readers in combination with current browsers (for example NVDA with Firefox, or VoiceOver with Safari).

## Known Limitations

- Rich text content entered by editors depends on the accessibility of the material they add, for example alt text on images and meaningful link text.
- Some Filament and third-party package components may not yet fully meet WCAG 2.2 AA. We track these and address them as they are found.

## Reporting Accessibility Barriers

If you encounter a barrier, please open an [issue](https://github.com/voorhof/stickler/issues) with the label `accessibility`, or e-mail David Carton at [david@notrac.be](mailto:david@notrac.be). Please describe the page or feature, the problem you experienced, and the assistive technology and browser you used. We aim to respond within 5 business days.
