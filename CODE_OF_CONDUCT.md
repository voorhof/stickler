# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in Stickler a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Accepting feedback and giving it in good faith.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to David Carton at [david@notrac.be](mailto:david@notrac.be). All reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially. Maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate, including warnings, temporary bans, or permanent removal from project spaces.
